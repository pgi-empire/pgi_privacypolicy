Privacy Policy

App: PGI Connect (com.pgi.attendance.mobile)
Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PGI ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the PGI Connect mobile application ("the App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how your organization uses the App, we may collect:

  • Account information: such as your name, employee/user ID, email address, or phone number.
  • Attendance data: such as check-in and check-out times, dates, and attendance status.
  • Device and technical information: such as device model, operating system version, app version, IP address, and crash or diagnostic logs.
  • Network information: the App checks your internet and Wi-Fi connection status so it can communicate with our servers.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone data unless this is stated otherwise in an update to this policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the attendance features of the App.
  • To authenticate users and keep accounts secure.
  • To generate attendance records and reports for your organization.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With your employer or organization that administers your account.
  • With service providers (such as hosting or analytics providers) who help us run the App and are bound to protect your data.
  • When required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and safety.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data is transmitted over the internet and stored on secure servers. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide the App's services, meet our organization's record-keeping requirements, or comply with legal obligations. After that, data is deleted or anonymized.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below. Because accounts are often managed by your employer, some requests may need to be directed to your organization's administrator.

7. Account and Data Deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data, email us at plantgreeninertiamanagement@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Account". We will process your request within a reasonable time, subject to legal retention requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services (for example Google Play services) that have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.
